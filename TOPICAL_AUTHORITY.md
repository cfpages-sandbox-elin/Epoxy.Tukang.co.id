## Evidence snapshot

- Audit date: 2026-07-24 (Asia/Jakarta).
- Repository: `cfpages-sandbox-elin/Epoxy.Tukang.co.id`, audited from shallow `main` at `5c63c7d`.
- The repository includes seven sitemap XML files. After normalizing absolute and relative `<loc>` values, the useful corpus is 34 public page routes.
- Service evidence in the sitemaps and archived navigation covers epoxy flooring, epoxy/PU coating, PU concrete, floor hardener, concrete injection, waterproofing, and epoxy 3D.
- Application pages name factories, warehouses, hospitals, hotels, basements, airports, and stations.
- WordPress utility/demo routes (`/20-2/`, `/404/`, `/about/`, `/contact/`, `/hello-world/`, `/sample-page/`, author/category/feed pages) are template noise and do not shape the authority map.
- The archived local root is a 404 shell, so no homepage claim is treated as evidence. Repeated footer superlatives and longevity claims are excluded.

## Position and boundary

The site should become a practical decision hub for owners, consultants, facility teams, and buyers commissioning resin-floor and adjacent concrete-protection work. It owns selection, substrate diagnosis, specification, application control, acceptance, maintenance, and procurement for the evidenced service families.

It does not own paint manufacturing, structural engineering design, chemical formulation recipes, DIY handling instructions for hazardous products, or unsupported performance/pricing promises. Location-swapped landing pages are excluded. Existing service routes remain commercial hubs; the catalog supplies informational support and must not duplicate their primary intent.

## Internal-link rules

1. EPX-01 is the main decision hub and links to the system-specific topics EPX-04, EPX-05, EPX-06, and EPX-08.
2. Every system article links back to EPX-02 and EPX-03 when substrate or preparation determines the answer.
3. Facility topics EPX-09 through EPX-11 link to system articles only after defining exposure, traffic, hygiene, and downtime requirements.
4. Defect articles in EPX-12 link to the relevant preparation, application, or maintenance article and avoid competing “general epoxy problem” pages.
5. Procurement articles in EPX-14 link to the chosen system, QA, and maintenance deliverables instead of making technical claims independently.
6. No title may be duplicated with only a city, building name, or generic “best/cheap” modifier.

## Evidence gates

- Do not publish numeric service life, thickness, coverage, curing time, slip value, chemical resistance, fire classification, VOC level, load capacity, temperature range, warranty, or price unless tied to a named product technical data sheet, test method, project specification, or documented measurement.
- Claims about hospital hygiene, food processing, airports, or other regulated facilities require the applicable owner specification and current standard; do not infer compliance from the material name.
- Moisture, adhesion, hardness, flatness, and slip test instructions must name the method, equipment, acceptance criterion, and responsible qualified party.
- Crack injection content must state that crack cause and structural significance require competent assessment before a repair material is chosen.
- Chemical-resistance recommendations require chemical identity, concentration, temperature, contact duration, cleaning regime, and manufacturer data.
- Project examples, before/after images, quantities, schedules, and defect diagnoses require provenance and permission. Decorative epoxy imagery is not proof of field performance.
- Commercial comparisons must use like-for-like scope: substrate repair, preparation grade, layer build-up, thickness, area, access, phasing, protection, testing, and handover.

## First bounded cluster

Publish EPX-01, EPX-02, EPX-03, EPX-04, EPX-12, and EPX-14 first. Together they create a complete selection-to-acceptance path while the specialist service and facility clusters await stronger product and project evidence.
